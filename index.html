Analog Circuit Simulator

Phase D: diode, switch, ideal op-amp, BJT, and MOSFET are now available, solved with Newton-Raphson iteration on top of the same DC/transient engine. All four planned phases are now in place.

–

Drag parts from the palette onto the grid. Click a terminal (blue dot) to start a wire, click another terminal to finish it. Click a component's body to select or drag it. With a component selected: R rotates, Delete removes it. Toggle Probe, then click a terminal to watch its voltage or a component body to watch its current — traces appear on the scope below once you run a transient. An AC Voltage Source's Amplitude/Phase double as the AC sweep's stimulus (its Frequency/Offset only apply to transient runs) — run an AC sweep to see a probed node's gain/phase vs. frequency on the Bode plot below the scope.
